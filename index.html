FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before you submit a case.

What cases can I submit to PATHwise?

PATHwise provides veterinary biopsy consultation for referring veterinarians. Submit the clinical history, biopsy site, and your diagnostic questions through the online case form.

Do I need an existing pathology report?

No. An existing pathology report can be included when available, but it is not required to submit a case.

What if I only have formalin-fixed tissue and no paraffin blocks yet?

If the biopsy has not yet been processed, the tissue will first need to be processed into paraffin blocks by a histology laboratory. You may use a local histology laboratory that you routinely work with.

For excisional biopsies where surgical margins need to be evaluated, please ensure that the margins are appropriately inked before trimming and processing.

Once the paraffin blocks are ready, submit the case through PATHwise and follow the instructions for sending the blocks to our designated histology and digital scanning facility in India.

Not sure how to proceed? Contact PATHwise before sending the tissue and we will help guide you through the process.

What material should I send?

Submit the case online first. You will then receive instructions for sending the paraffin block(s) to our designated facility in India for slide preparation and digital scanning.

Physical biopsy material does not need to be shipped outside India. The digital slides are reviewed remotely by the PATHwise veterinary pathologist.

How long does a consultation take?

Typical turnaround is 3–5 business days after successful scanning. Cases requiring additional material or ancillary testing may take longer.

Can I discuss the case with the pathologist?

Yes. Referring veterinarians can contact PATHwise to discuss the pathology findings and questions related to the consultation report.
